I'm Tyler Ostern, founder of Auctorix. I started this studio in Coos Bay because too much marketing runs on instinct nobody goes back and checks. Every engagement starts by finding what's actually working for your business — then building on it.

The name comes from the Latin auctor — the originator, the one whose authority a thing is built on. The mark is two triangles braced against each other: a steady foundation and a sharp leading edge.

I also work inside a car dealership, which is where our dealership tools come from. Either way, you'll be working with me directly.
