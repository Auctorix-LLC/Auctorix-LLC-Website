One platform

Every desk in the store, on the same page.

Today a customer lives in five systems that don't talk to each other, and your team is the glue. The Auctorix Dealer Platform puts it all on one database, so a lead, a deal and a car are the same record everywhere.

CRM

Leads that don't fall through

Every lead, call and visit on one timeline, with real follow-up tasks and reminders — not just a log nobody reads.

DMS

Deals without double entry

The customer you worked in the CRM is the customer on the deal. No re-keying names, addresses or trades from one screen to the next.

IMS

Inventory and appraisals

Every unit, photo and price in one place, with book values built into appraisals — and feeds to your website and Marketplace straight from the source.

SMS
